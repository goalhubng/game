# Sugar Scramble Deluxe

A single-file match-3 game (`index.html`) with a 1,000-stage tour across Nigeria. Open the file in a browser to play; progress is saved in `localStorage`.

## How to play
- Tap two neighbouring candies, or swipe one, to swap them and line up 3+ of the same colour.
- **Match 4** → striped candy (clears a row or column). **L/T shape** → wrapped candy (clears a 3×3 area). **Match 5** → colour bomb (swap it with a candy to clear that whole colour).
- Each stage has a points target plus an objective: clear the green **jelly** tiles, the brown **chocolate** tiles, both, or beat a **boss** every 10th stage.
- **Traffic cones** block cells and break after two hits from nearby matches. **Cages** lock a candy in place until it is matched.
- Leftover moves turn into bonus points (Sugar Rush). Out of moves? Buy +5 moves to keep your life.
- Lives refill one every 20 minutes. Leaving a stage after making a move costs a life.

## Extras
Quick Blitz (60-second score rush), daily Lucky Spin, 7-day Transit Pass, bounties, trophies, a souvenir scrapbook and the Danfo garage (paint jobs, horns and perks).
