How to play

  • 🟢 Press on the left green dot and draw a line to the other one.
  • 🔴 Don't touch a ball. Moving balls can also hit your line, so draw fast.
  • ⭕ Keep your line inside the circle.
  • 🖊️ You only have so much ink. Finish at or below the par mark for a star.
  • 💎 Pick up every gem for a star.
  • 💡 Each star raises your IQ. You need all 30 stars to reach 161.
